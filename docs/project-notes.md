## Context

This was the completed housing-demand final project for ECON 349 Industrial Economics in Spring 2026. The assignment used a hypothetical Lakeside Property Group scenario and supplied survey data.

It is separate from my earlier proposed fitness-add-on survey and from Admissions Architects' simulated pricing-survey work.

## My work and assistance

I authored the final report. I used AI only for assistance with the software, not to write the original report prose. This does not claim the implementation was entirely unaided.

The report and saved Stata session support the data preparation, two conditional-logit specifications, willingness-to-pay discussion, package recommendations, and proposed survey improvements summarized here.

The retrospective repository write-up was prepared with AI assistance. It does not change original authorship or represent a newly completed analysis.

## Interpretation

Lakeside is a classroom scenario, not a real client. I did not collect the supplied survey. The study has 50 respondents, not 1,000.

Recommendations are proposals, not measured business outcomes. Future learning or later portfolio writing does not alter the historical work.
